# Top-five adversarial judge tournament

Five independently framed reviewer perspectives were simulated against the [official rubric](../context/JUDGING_CRITERIA.md). Judgment is provisional. Initial incident examples and all outcomes below are hypothetical. [Scores and gates](../strategy/PROBLEM_SELECTION.md#management-open-innovation)

| Judge perspective | AccessRelay | BenchShift | ExamRelay | DormPivot | ReUse Dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Agentic AI expert | Why does the model choose a source? Show an ambiguous fixture where it requests scope instead of proposing a move. | Why use AI for allocation? Use a rules solver for calendar math and AI for evidence mapping and verification requests. | Can rules handle accommodation checks? Yes. Agent value is interpreting disruption and asking for the missing fact. | Is it a notification bot? Verify unit coverage and alternate capacity before routing. | Is it semantic search? Show how ambiguous condition starts an inspection and a failed pickup triggers a replan. |

**Head-to-head.** AccessRelay beats BenchShift on the audience’s ability to see the consequence immediately and on independent evidence for the university workflow. BenchShift beats ExamRelay on the first tie-breaker because a verified mock instrument swap has fewer protected constraints. DormPivot is simpler to illustrate than ReUse Dispatch, but its scope must produce verified service coverage. ReUse Dispatch has an interesting approval mechanism and narrower judged impact than the top three. Only AccessRelay combines a short opening hook, credible institutional evidence, robust demo path and a very narrow implementation.

**Most likely rejection:** the PoC is missing, or judges see nothing beyond room booking. Correct first by confirming the rubric and build window with organizers. If permitted, show two distinct visible changes in the toy state: one candidate becoming invalid, and a different accessible booking becoming active after approval. Show the human acknowledgment and a precise explanation of what the model interpreted. If only slides are permitted, use a clearly labeled storyboard and state that the prototype and metrics are planned.
