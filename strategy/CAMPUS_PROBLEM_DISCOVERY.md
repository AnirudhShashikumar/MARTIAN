# Campus problem discovery

The research establishes that these workflows occur at universities. Incident frequency at BMSITM is unknown. Documented procedures, incident examples and incumbent capabilities are evidence of workflows and possible gaps. They do not establish market demand or performance improvement. **Highest-evidence gap.** A facilities outage can create a cross-office obligation that ends only when an accessible class plan is enacted and acknowledged. Prevalence at BMSITM, incident timing and willingness to adopt remain open. In the deck, use documented workflows and a named synthetic scenario. If permitted, ask a coordinator to validate the handoff steps without collecting incident records or personal data.
