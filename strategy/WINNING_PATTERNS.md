# Winning innovation patterns

The award sample is descriptive. A pattern appearing in award recipients supports testing it in our pitch. It does not establish that the pattern caused the award. [Winner database](../references/research/HACKATHON_WINNERS.md)

| Pattern | Why it matters and where it transfers | Overnight original implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resolve the exception through a verified action | SalesShortcut and Province make the output tangible. A campus relocation also needs a final booking and an accountable handoff. | Approve a proposal, update a synthetic schedule, then read the booking and notice status back. |
| Translate ambiguous inputs into a decision | AegisAgent and Meeting Tasks Agent connect noisy information to an operational next step. A partial facilities note can leave the affected building or time unclear. | Let one model choose a lookup or clarification. Require source IDs and send unsafe ambiguity to a coordinator. |
| Run constraint checks outside the model | VendorGuard and RiskWise make policy and evidence inspectable. Accessibility, capacity and time conflicts are enforceable campus constraints. | Model proposes an action and deterministic code validates it. Show one rejected candidate and its reason. |
| Replan after a resource changes | GreenOps and Eco-Metropolis show decisions within a changing model of available resources. Shared equipment and available rooms can change mid-plan. | Use a new room-state version to invalidate approval, then present the next valid option. |
| Show accessibility through task completion | FROM YOUR EYES, Edith and Mira help audiences see a specific access barrier and what happens after assistance. | Show a previously inaccessible class becoming reachable for the whole class without student identities in the workflow. |
| Make human ownership and uncertainty visible | Spoon Share uses human verification. Raycaster grounds recommendations in evidence. The registrar remains responsible for changes and the instructor confirms the handoff. | Record who approved which plan version, show pending acknowledgment, and route an unresolved case to the owner. |
| Demonstrate stable behavior when a dependency fails | Saheli makes offline usefulness part of its product story. An operational tool must degrade without silently guessing. | Keep a structured coordinator intake and deterministic planner for a timeout. Label that path as manual intake. |

The transferable mechanism is a visible operational recovery with a reason to use model judgment and a clear point at which rules or a human take over. Campus-specific accessibility, approval policy, revalidation and notification acknowledgment make AccessRelay an original scoped application.
