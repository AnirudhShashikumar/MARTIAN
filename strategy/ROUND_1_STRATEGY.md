# Round 1 top-10 strategy

Make one campus workflow the thread of the pitch. Show the current bottleneck, the specific decision an agent improves, the action it is allowed to take, how it verifies the outcome, and what the team can demonstrate by morning. Anchor differentiation in an observed workflow gap and assess success against a practical baseline.

## Execution budget

Minutes below are usable work time. Recalculate from the actual arrival time and the earliest confirmed deck deadline or judging slot, with lunch excluded where applicable.

| Task | Full · 160 min | Compressed · 100 min | Emergency · 60 min |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Statement analysis and user selection | 15 | 10 | 7 |
| Research and competitive gap | 15 | 10 | 5 |
| Three approaches and concept refinement | 20 | 10 | 6 |
| Agent workflow, architecture and feasibility | 20 | 15 | 9 |
| Narrative and slide content | 25 | 15 | 9 |
| Deck production and diagrams | 35 | 20 | 12 |
| Panel review and corrections | 15 | 10 | 6 |
| Rehearsal and final verification | 15 | 10 | 6 |
| **Total** | **160** | **100** | **60** |

Select the closest workable budget and scale it to the time available. Protect the upload and rehearsal block. If time tightens, prioritize exact problem fit, the agent’s decision and verification loop, architecture, differentiation and a complete readable deck. Use the remaining time for Q&A and design polish.

## Ownership and handoffs

1. **Selection lead** records source requirements, ranks all eligible statements and requests the user's decision. In parallel, confirm presentation rules and assign work.
2. **Research and product** verify the campus workflow and comparison, then present three genuinely different concepts. Capture the selected concept, the strongest reason to shortlist it and the main delivery risk.
3. **Architecture and validation** establish the smallest demonstrable flow, permissions, failure path, evidence plan and overnight build sequence. Share the diagrams and dependency risks with the deck owner as soon as stable.
4. **Deck and pitch** assemble the presentation while research and architecture finish. Keep a single editor responsible for the slide master and export. Use the [presentation workflow](../presentation/SLIDE_PLAN.md).
5. **Review and submit** using the [judge review](../evaluation/JUDGE_REVIEW.md). Fix rejection risks first, then rehearse, reopen the export and verify submission receipt. Log decisions and any unresolved assumptions.

Assign lanes to available team members once roster and strengths are known. A major strategy change or final problem selection requires user approval. Preserve time while that decision is pending by confirming logistics and preparing the review outline.
