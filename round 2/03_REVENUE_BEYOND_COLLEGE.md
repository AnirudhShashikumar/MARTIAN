## 1. The Commercial Thesis: "Software-First Vision Ops Over Existing CCTV"

College campuses are simply **micro-cities** — they share the exact same operational pain points as IT parks, hospitals, shopping malls, gated townships, and municipal transit hubs:
1. **Mixed two-wheeler + four-wheeler gate surges** with zero live visibility into open bays or overflow routing.
2. **Massive silent electricity waste** in intermittently occupied rooms (conference rooms, clinics, waiting lounges, classrooms, coworking pods) where appliances run hours after people leave.

**Why incumbents leave 85% of Indian facilities unserved:**
- Turnkey ANPR + boom-barrier + loop-sensor hardware costs **₹1.2L–₹1.8L per lane** (and ultrasonic per-bay sensors cost lakhs more in cabling).
- Commercial Integrated BMS (IBMS) retrofits cost **₹35–₹80+ per sq. ft.** (₹35L–₹80L for a 100,000 sq. ft. commercial building!).

**CampusFlow's Commercial Wedge:** Over **90% of commercial buildings, tech parks, hospitals, and RWAs in India already have CCTV cameras** at their gates and common rooms. CampusFlow deploys as a **software-first Edge AI + Cloud SaaS layer** on top of existing IP/RTSP cameras and ₹1,500 smart relays — slashing upfront CapEx by **~80–90%** and making ROI immediate.

### Likely Judge Questions & Winning Answers
- **Q: "Why won't Park+ or MyGate just copy this?"**  
  **A:** Park+ and MyGate are **single-silo consumer/gate apps** tied to FASTag stickers or heavy hardware installations. None of them unify **gate mobility + room occupancy energy Auto-OFF + shared facility booking** under one explainable agentic control plane that runs over existing CCTV. Plus, our **channel-partner model** empowers local CCTV integrators instead of competing with them.
- **Q: "What makes your gross margin ~82% when AI video processing is expensive?"**  
  **A:** **Edge inference.** Streaming 24/7 video to AWS/GCP GPUs destroys SaaS margins. By running lightweight quantized YOLOv8n (`imgsz=416`) on a single on-prem edge box inside the customer's LAN, our cloud only receives <1 KB JSON events — keeping cloud COGS under ₹2,000/site/month.
- **Q: "How do you sell to slow-moving institutions or corporates?"**  
  **A:** **Zero-CapEx 14-day pilot.** Because we plug into their existing RTSP camera streams with zero civil work or road digging, a facility head can see real parking & energy-waste logs in 48 hours and justify the SaaS fee directly from their existing monthly electricity budget.
