# Pitch script

Once the concept and time limit are known, write the opening around a concrete campus scenario, tie each transition to a judge takeaway, and close with what the team will prove in Round 2. Add a concise elevator pitch and assign speaker handoffs.

Rehearse aloud against the organizer time limit, leaving a margin for transitions. Move details that interrupt the flow into Q&A. Record the rehearsed run time and export version.
