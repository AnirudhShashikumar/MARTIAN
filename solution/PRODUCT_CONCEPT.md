# CampusFlow product concept

**One-line promise.** Turn a last-minute campus schedule change into verified parking and room decisions.

**Primary user.** An operations coordinator can see what changed and what remains unresolved. Parking staff authorize overflow, facilities staff authorize room-power changes and timetable staff own the booking. Students, staff and approved visitors benefit from usable capacity and clear decisions.

**Core problem.** A late session move or delay can alter the arrival window and release an old room. A gate report, academic schedule and occupancy reading each answer only part of the question. Coordinating the two responses involves human interpretation and fresh checks at the time of action.

**Journey.** A synthetic staff note announces a lab induction move and delay. CampusFlow resolves the session record and its revised window, checks the gate and former room, then proposes a time-limited overflow allocation and a separate occupancy decision. Staff authorize the applicable branch. When a fresh frame shows occupancy, the room branch is held and rechecked independently. A later vacant sequence can produce a fresh approved room action. The case displays applied, verified, pending and escalated branches.

**MVP.** One late change, one gate, one ordinary lot per vehicle class, one overflow zone, one old classroom, one alternate lab and one policy-controlled model. Extend the reusable availability interface with sample lab, library-seat and sports-equipment rows. Keep a complete live handoff for parking and simulated room devices.

| Primary tab | User-facing capabilities | Approval boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| College Resource Allocation | Classroom and lab availability, library seat capacity, sports-equipment availability and checkout, and occupancy-informed mock classroom device controls. | Facilities approves the affected device action after vacancy, booking and override checks. |
| Vehicle Fleet Management | Car and bike slot availability, assignment by class, gate entry and exit timestamps, space release and time-limited overflow. | Parking staff approve access to designated temporary overflow. |

Each tab exposes its current state and shares the incident summary and audit. The agent links both to the same confirmed schedule while retaining independent decisions and outcomes.

**Impact to measure.** Time to verified parking and room resolution, valid allocations, unsafe actions blocked and verified-vacant device-on minutes.
