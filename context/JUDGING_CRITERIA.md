# Judging criteria

Round 1 rubric received November 25, 2026. It has four equally weighted criteria, each worth up to 5 marks, for a total of 20. [Organizer rubric](../references/official/Martian%201.0%20Round%201%20Evaluation%20Rubric.pdf)

| Criterion | Evidence needed for top marks | AccessRelay presentation mapping |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proof of Concept | Reliable live run of working code completing the core user flow | Slide 5 and live walkthrough, incident to committed mock room booking and loan, then receipt and instructor acknowledgment |
| Technical Research | Defensible stack, clear architecture and research into libraries, latency and stability | Slide 4, model tools, room and assessment checks, versioned approval, transactional write and latency budget |
| Market & Industry | Defined users, specific incumbent comparison and quantified evidence of an unmet need | Slides 2–3, documented room, equipment and assessment workflows, named tools, test metrics and limits of local quantification |
| Feasibility & Execution | Precise milestones, realistic scope, testing and contingency | Slides 5–6, one assessment and synthetic ledger on a laptop, with optional hardware simulation |

The rubric schedules **3 minutes for a working prototype walkthrough, architecture and market rationale, plus 3 minutes for jury Q&A**. Ties are decided by PoC, then Technical Research, then Feasibility & Execution, then Market & Industry. Bands are 5 exemplary, 4 proficient, 2–3 developing and 0–1 needs improvement.
