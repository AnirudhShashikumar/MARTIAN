## Security · Track 1 · Comms Guardian

**Problem**

Our college runs a large network that connects labs, hostels, classrooms, admin offices and guest Wi-Fi. One network head or analyst oversees all of it. They cannot watch thousands of devices and endless activity around the clock, so threats can go unnoticed until real damage is done. Warnings are also often hard to understand and arrive too late to act on.

**Task**

Build an AI agent that supports the network head. It should:

- monitor a simulated campus network that you set up yourself,
- detect suspicious or harmful activity,
- explain what it found in clear language,
- take or recommend a suitable response.

In your demo, simulate an attack on your own network and show your agent catching and responding to it.

**Intake note.** User is the network lead. The campus network and telemetry are team-created. The brief leaves the simulated scenario and response policy to the team under the Security rules.

## Security · Track 2 · Mission Portal Shield

**Problem**

The college runs several websites, such as results, admissions, attendance and library portals. These hold personal data about students and staff, which makes them attractive targets. A website with weak protection can leak records, be misused, or go down at the worst possible moment. Many sites also lack someone watching them continuously.

**Task**

Build an AI agent that protects a college-style website. You should:

- create your own website that resembles a campus portal, using fake data only,
- build an agent that defends it,
- simulate an attack on your own website,
- show how your agent handles that attack.

Your demo should make the difference clear: what happens without your agent, and what happens with it.

**Intake note.** Users include portal operators and people relying on the portal. Deliverables include the team-created site, defensive agent and before/after comparison. The portal type is unspecified.

## Security · Track 3 · Life-Support Watchdog

**Problem**

Smart devices quietly keep the campus running: cameras, attendance scanners, air-quality sensors, smart meters, door locks and lab equipment. They are often poorly secured, rarely updated and seldom monitored. A single compromised device can be used to spy, to feed false information, or to attack the rest of the network, and it can affect people's safety and wellbeing.

**Task**

Build an AI agent that secures a group of campus IoT devices. You should:

- set up a group of devices in your own lab (simulated or real hardware),
- build an agent that keeps watch over them and protects the system,
- simulate an attack on your own devices,
- show how your agent detects and responds to it.

**Intake note.** Likely user is a campus facilities or device operator. Simulated devices satisfy the setup requirement. Agree on the simulated scenario and story with the organizers if demo expectations are further specified.

## Management · Track 1 · Arena Command

**Problem**

The sports department runs many events through the year, often twenty or more, and each one needs fixtures, venues, officials, registrations, results and reports. They also select and send students to competitions, handle sponsorship and fund requests, and keep records of winners and achievements. All of this is scattered across messages and papers, which makes it slow to manage and easy to lose.

**Task**

Build an AI agent that supports the sports department. It should:

- help plan and run a multi-event sports calendar, including registrations, schedules and clashes,
- help manage selection, permissions and sponsorship or fund requests for students going to competitions,
- keep records of winners and achievements and prepare reports from them.

In your demo, show your agent handling a realistic run of sports events from start to finish.

**Intake note.** Users are sports staff, event organizers and student competitors. The statement spans planning, approvals and reporting. Use team-created sample data and treat the actual selection and approval policies as unspecified.

## Management · Track 2 · Galley Control

**Problem**

Students queue to order and pay at the different food outlets on campus, and the people running those outlets deal with cash handling, wrong orders, cancellations and refund disputes. Neither side has a clear, trusted record of what was ordered, paid or refunded.

**Task**

Build an AI agent for campus food ordering and payments. You should:

- create an ordering flow where a student picks an item, pays, and receives a QR code to scan at the counter to collect their food,
- build an agent that handles cancellations, refunds and failed payments within rules the outlet sets, and escalates unclear cases to a human,
- have the agent spot misuse, such as repeated refunds or a QR code used twice, and give the outlet a clear summary of orders, payments and issues.

Payments must be simulated or use a sandbox gateway. Your demo should show the full journey, from order to payment to collection, including a cancellation and a refund.

**Intake note.** Users are students and outlet operators. Payment and collection state, exception handling and operator reporting are deliverables. Outlet rules are unspecified and must be made explicit in a sample scenario.

## Management · Track 3 · Crew Sync

**Problem**

The college has many clubs. Their events, budgets, approvals and members are managed separately, so dates and venues clash, collaborations are missed, and some clubs slowly go quiet.

**Task**

Build an AI agent that helps the clubs work in harmony. It should:

- keep a shared view of club events and flag clashes,
- suggest collaborations between clubs and match volunteers to events,
- notice when a club is losing activity or members, suggest how to revive it, and help prepare event reports.

In your demo, show your agent solving a realistic situation involving several clubs.

**Intake note.** Likely users are club leads and coordinators. The core demo can center on one clash, but collaboration, volunteer matching, activity signals and reporting remain in the brief. Use team-created sample club data.
